About Grandyra

Grandyra is a branding and communications house in Dhaka. We start by asking why: why this business, why now, why would anyone choose it. Then we build the brand, the campaign and the experience that answer it. Strategy first. Then the beautiful part.

How we think

Four rules
we work by

Strategy before aesthetics

We work out the business problem before we shape anything visual. Design should be an answer, not a guess.

Substance over noise

In a cluttered market we practise restraint. Every element has to earn its place.

Longevity over trends

Trends expire. We build visual systems meant to hold their value for years, not seasons.

Craft over convenience

From type scales to venue signage, the details get the time they need.

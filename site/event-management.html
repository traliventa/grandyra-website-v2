01 — The problem

Many events are well organised and instantly forgettable — the same stage, the same backdrop, the same running order.

We design events as brand experiences: one idea carried through the invitation, the space, the programme and what guests take home.

02 — How we approach it

  1. 01

    Concept

    The idea and the feeling the event should leave behind.

  2. 02

    Design

    Space, stage, signage and every touchpoint guests see.

  3. 03

    Produce

    Vendors, logistics, technical production and rehearsals.

  4. 04

    Run

    On-site management, so you can be a host, not a project manager.

04 — Questions

Asked often

What kinds of events do you produce?+

Brand and product launches, tournaments, markets, expos and brand activations. See Real Estate Futsal 2026 and Seheri Nights in our work.

Do you work outside Dhaka?+

Our studio is in Dhaka, and we are happy to travel for the right project. Tell us where it is and we will plan around it.

How far in advance should we book?+

The earlier the better. Larger events need a few months. Smaller activations can move faster.

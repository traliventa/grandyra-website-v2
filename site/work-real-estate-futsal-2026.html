Case study

Real Estate
Futsal 2026

Grandyra was the official creative partner for the tournament, shaping how it looked, from the trophy to the court.

Role
Official creative partner
Venue
ISD, Dhaka
When
October 2026
Scope
Identity, trophies, venue, campaign

The identity

A tournament with its own mark

The official emblem captures the drive of the industry and the heart of futsal. Its line, “Built from the skyline, forged for the game.”, gave sixteen teams a tournament with an identity of its own.

Kits, passes and awards

One system, down to the lanyard

Accreditation, player kits and awards all carried the same identity, so the tournament looked like one thing from the lanyard to the crystal award.

Custom trophies and hardware

Made for the final moment

The champions’ trophy was designed with the event branding around it, so the winning photograph carries the identity.

Venue branding

Branding the court

Court-side banners and wall graphics at ISD carried the identity into the space, for the players and the crowd.

Digital and social campaign

Built for the feed and the big screen

Social creatives and live-screening graphics shared the same look as everything on the court.

On the turf

Unforgettable moments

The branding at work with teams, organisers and the crowd, and the Grandyra team on the day.
